# UTi260B catalog imagery

Source: https://uni-tnm.com/product/meter/html/UTi260B.html

Branded product flyer: https://damoatool02.cafe24.com/pdf//Uni-T/Product%20Flyer--UTi260B.pdf

The inspected single-page flyer contains two product views. The CI script embeds cropped PNGs in the offline guide; no runtime network access is required. No generated thermal image is represented as a measurement. Product imagery remains subject to the respective rights holders' terms; redistribution rights have not been verified. The app is independent, not an official UNI-T product.

If the source is unavailable or its model text does not match, the build stops rather than silently substituting other equipment. Device-menu photos have not been added.
